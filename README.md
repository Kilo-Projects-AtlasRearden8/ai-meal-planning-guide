# AI Meal Planning Guide

Plan a week of meals from what's already in your pantry — using free AI tools, step by step, in plain English.

Brought to you by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — beginner-friendly AI education, books, and practical tools.

Dylan Asdale, founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC, is a registered nurse turned AI author. His book *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io explain AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending. This guide follows that same spirit.

## Who this is for

Anyone who opens the fridge and wonders, "What can I make with this?" — especially:

- People on a grocery budget who want to waste less food
- Cooks juggling dietary restrictions (gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, diabetes-friendly, low-sodium, and more)
- Beginners who have never used an AI chat tool before
- Nurses, shift workers, and busy folks who want low-energy, low-stress meal planning

## Quick start

**Option A — Read the Markdown manual:** Open `docs/index.md` for the table of contents, or read any section file directly.

**Option B — Use the website:** Open `site/index.html` in any web browser. Everything works offline once downloaded — nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, nothing phones home.

**Option C — Print the templates:** Open `site/templates/weekly-meal-plan.html` or `site/templates/grocery-list.html` and press Ctrl+P / Cmd+P.

## Medical disclaimer

This guide is for education and general meal-planning ideas only. It is not medical advice, nutrition counseling, or a substitute for care from a registered dietitian or your healthcare team. If you have a medical condition that affects what you can eat, talk to your doctor or dietitian before making big changes to your diet.
