### How to use it

1. List your pantry ingredients first.
2. Ask the AI for a menu (Section 06, Prompt 1).
3. Write each dinner in the grid.
4. Mark which nights are leftovers.
5. Keep it on the fridge.

## Printable HTML versions

- `site/templates/weekly-meal-plan.html` — clean, printer-friendly weekly planner
- `site/templates/grocery-list.html` — sectioned grocery list
- Open either in your browser and press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P) to print

## Tips

- Print in black and white to save ink
- Keep a blank pad on the fridge for running pantry notes
- Take a photo of your completed planner with your phone so you have it at the store
