# 05. Budget-Friendly Recipes

Eating well on a budget means cooking at home, using what you have, and wasting less. AI can suggest cheap meals, but it doesn't know your local prices — treat its cost guesses as estimates.

## Cheap staple ingredients (keep these on hand)

- Rice, pasta, oats, bread, tortillas
- Dried or canned beans, lentils, chickpeas
- Eggs
- Canned tomatoes, tomato paste, marinara
- Frozen vegetables and fruit
- Peanut butter
- Onions, garlic, carrots, cabbage, potatoes (cheap fresh produce that lasts)
- Bananas
- Chicken thighs (usually cheaper than breasts)
- Canned tuna or salmon

## Budget principles AI can help with

1. **Use up what you have** before buying more (pantry-first planning)
2. **Plan leftovers** — cook once, eat twice
3. **Stretch meat** with beans, rice, or vegetables
4. **Buy store brands**
5. **Shop the frozen aisle** — frozen produce is often cheaper and lasts longer

## Copy-and-paste budget prompts

### Cheap meals from pantry staples

> Give me 7 dinners that cost under $[AMOUNT] total for [NUMBER] people, using cheap staples like rice, beans, eggs, pasta, canned tomatoes, and frozen vegetables. Include a rough cost per meal and a grocery list.

### Stretch expensive ingredients

> I have 1 lb of ground beef. How can I stretch it into 4 meals using beans, rice, or vegetables? Give me recipes and a grocery list.

### Leftover remix

> I have leftover [FOOD]. Give me 3 ways to turn it into a different meal tomorrow so it doesn't feel like eating the same thing twice.

### Low-cost snacks and breakfasts

> Give me 5 breakfasts and 5 snacks under $[AMOUNT] each using oats, eggs, peanut butter, bananas, and bread.

## Example: $40 week for 2 people

This is a *sample* — your local prices will differ.

| Day | Dinner | Rough cost |
|-----|--------|------------|
| Mon | Rice & beans with frozen vegetables | $2 |
| Tue | Pasta with marinara & canned tuna | $3 |
| Wed | Eggs & toast with frozen vegetables | $2 |
| Thu | Chicken thighs, rice, broccoli | $6 |
| Fri | Bean & cheese quesadillas | $3 |
| Sat | Pasta with marinara (leftovers) | $0 |
| Sun | Breakfast for dinner (eggs, toast) | $2 |

Total groceries for the week: about $40, plus pantry staples you already own.

## Try this

> I have $[AMOUNT] for groceries this week for [NUMBER] people. I already have [PANTRY LIST]. Plan [NUMBER] dinners and breakfasts, give me a grocery list, and keep the total under $[AMOUNT]. Tell me which meals make good leftovers.
