# 04. Cooking with Dietary Restrictions

AI is good at adapting recipes, but it is not a dietitian. Always double-check with your healthcare team if a medical condition is involved.

## What AI can help with

- Gluten-free (wheat-free) meals
- Dairy-free / lactose-free
- Vegetarian and vegan
- Low-sodium
- Diabetes-friendly (carbohydrate awareness)
- Allergy swaps (nuts, eggs, soy, shellfish)
- Low-energy / low-prep cooking (ADHD-friendly, limited spoons)

## How to ask

Tell the AI your restrictions up front, in every new chat:

> I need gluten-free, dairy-free dinners for 2 people. I have chicken thighs, rice, frozen vegetables, and canned tomatoes. Plan 4 dinners and flag any ingredient that might contain gluten or dairy.

## Safe prompting habits

- Say "I need gluten-free" rather than "I think I can't eat gluten." AI follows direct instructions better.
- Ask it to flag uncertain ingredients: "Tell me if any ingredient commonly contains hidden gluten."
- Never rely on AI alone for severe allergies. Read every label yourself.
- Ask for swaps, not just removals: "Instead of cheese, what can I use for creaminess?"

## Example: diabetes-friendly prompt

> Plan 5 dinners for 2 adults. Keep meals diabetes-friendly: moderate carbohydrates, plenty of non-starchy vegetables, protein with each meal. I have chicken, eggs, canned beans, frozen vegetables, rice, and pasta. Use smaller portions of rice and pasta. List approximate carb counts per serving if you can, but note that I should verify with my dietitian.

## Example: low-energy / ADHD-friendly prompt

> I have low energy today. Plan 3 dinners that use one pan or one sheet tray, minimal chopping, and leftovers for the next day's lunch. I have eggs, bread, canned beans, and frozen vegetables.

## Important cautions

- AI can get carb counts, sodium counts, and allergy info wrong. Treat its numbers as rough estimates.
- "Gluten-free" from an AI doesn't mean a product is certified gluten-free. Check labels.
- If you have a life-threatening allergy, do not trust an AI to clear a food as safe.

## Try this

> I need [RESTRICTION, e.g. dairy-free and gluten-free] dinners for [NUMBER] people. I have [PANTRY LIST]. Plan [NUMBER] meals, flag anything that might violate my restrictions, and suggest safe swaps. Remind me to check labels myself.
