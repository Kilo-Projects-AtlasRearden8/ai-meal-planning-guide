03. Building Grocery Lists

A good grocery list saves money and time: you buy only what you need, organized so you don't backtrack through the store.

Step 1: Ask the AI for a list

Once you have a menu, ask the AI to turn it into a shopping list.

Copy-and-paste prompt

Based on this weekly menu, make me a grocery list of only the ingredients I don't already have at home.

I already have: [list what you have]

Menu:
[paste your menu]

Please organize the list by grocery store section:
- Produce
- Meat / protein
- Dairy & eggs
- Canned & jarred goods
- Dry goods / grains
- Frozen
- Bakery
- Other

Include rough quantities for [NUMBER] people.

Step 2: Check your pantry first

Before you shop, look at what you actually have. The AI doesn't know if you have half an onion or a full jar of oregano. Cross off anything you already own.

Step 3: Estimate the cost

Free AI can't see your store's prices, but it can guess. Ask:

Based on average U.S. grocery prices, roughly how much would this list cost?

Then check your store's app or flyer. If it's over budget, ask the AI:

This is over my $[AMOUNT] budget. Suggest cheaper swaps for the most expensive items.

Step 4: Stick to the list

  • Shop after eating (you buy less when you're not hungry)
  • Buy store brands — they're usually cheaper and taste the same
  • Buy frozen vegetables and fruit (they last longer and cost less)
  • Skip pre-cut produce — whole carrots cost less than carrot sticks

Example grocery list

From the Section 02 menu:

SectionItems
Produce2 heads broccoli (frozen is fine), 1 bag onions, 1 head garlic
Meat / protein2 lbs chicken thighs, 1 can black beans (if you ran out)
Dairy & eggs1 dozen eggs
Dry goods1 bag rice, 1 box pasta, 1 jar marinara sauce
OtherOlive oil, salt, pepper (if low)

Try this

Here's my menu: [paste menu]. I already have [paste pantry list]. Make a grocery list organized by store section for [number] people, and flag anything that might be expensive so I can swap it.
