07. Printable Templates

Print these out and fill them in by hand, or type into them before printing. Printer-friendly HTML versions are in site/templates/ — open them in your browser and press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P) to print.

How to use it

  1. List your pantry ingredients first.
  2. Ask the AI for a menu (Section 06, Prompt 1).
  3. Write each dinner in the grid.
  4. Mark which nights are leftovers.
  5. Keep it on the fridge.
