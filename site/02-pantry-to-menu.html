02. From Pantry to Weekly Menu

The goal: start with what you already have, end with a week of dinners (and maybe breakfasts and lunches) that use those ingredients first — less waste, less spending.

Step 1: List what you have

You don't need to be precise. A rough list is fine. Include:

  • Proteins: chicken, ground beef, eggs, tofu, canned beans, canned tuna
  • Grains: rice, pasta, bread, tortillas, oats, quinoa
  • Canned/jarred: tomatoes, marinara, soup, broth, peanut butter
  • Frozen: vegetables, fruit, fish fillets, pizza dough
  • Fresh: vegetables, fruit, dairy (use these first — they spoil fastest)
  • Pantry basics: oil, salt, pepper, garlic, onions, spices

Write it down on paper or type it into your phone notes. You'll paste it into the AI chat.

Step 2: Tell the AI your constraints

A good pantry-to-menu prompt includes:

  1. Your ingredient list
  2. How many people and which meals (dinners? breakfasts too?)
  3. Time you want to spend cooking
  4. Budget target (optional)
  5. Foods you dislike or can't eat

Step 3: Ask for the menu

Copy-and-paste prompt

I want to plan [NUMBER] dinners for [PEOPLE] people using ingredients I already have.

Here's what I have: [PASTE YOUR PANTRY LIST]

Please suggest a weekly menu. For each meal, tell me:
- What I cook
- Which pantry ingredients it uses
- Approximate cook time
- One simple instruction summary

Keep recipes simple (under 45 minutes, minimal cleanup). I have a budget of about $[AMOUNT] for any extra groceries.

Example with real values

I want to plan 5 dinners for 2 people using ingredients I already have.

Here's what I have: chicken thighs, rice, canned black beans, frozen broccoli, eggs, pasta, jarred marinara, onions, garlic, olive oil, salt, pepper.

Please suggest a weekly menu. For each meal, tell me what I cook, which pantry ingredients it uses, approximate cook time, and one simple instruction summary. Keep recipes simple (under 45 minutes, minimal cleanup). I have a budget of about $40 for any extra groceries.

Step 4: Review and revise

If the AI suggests something weird ("make chicken ice cream"), just say so:

That doesn't sound right. Can you swap Wednesday's meal for something more normal, and use the black beans?

You can ask for:

  • "Make it faster"
  • "Use less cleanup (one pan or one pot)"
  • "Kid-friendly"
  • "No recipes I need the oven for"
  • "Use up the spinach before it goes bad"

Step 5: Lock it in

Once you like the menu, write it down (or print the template in Section 07). A simple weekly grid:

DayDinnerMain pantry ingredients used
MonPasta with marinara & broccoliPasta, marinara, frozen broccoli, onion, garlic
TueChicken & rice bowlChicken thighs, rice, broccoli
WedBean & rice tacosBlack beans, rice, tortillas, onion
ThuPasta & eggsPasta, eggs, marinara
FriLeftovers nightAnything from earlier in the week

Common pantry starter combos

If your cupboards are nearly bare, these cheap staples unlock hundreds of meals:

  • Rice + canned beans + frozen vegetables
  • Pasta + jarred marinara + canned tuna or beans
  • Eggs + toast + frozen vegetables
  • Tortillas + beans + cheese + salsa
  • Oats + peanut butter + banana

Try this

Open a free AI chat and paste:

I have [your pantry list]. Plan 4 dinners for [number] people using mostly what I have. Keep each under 30 minutes. Tell me what to buy if anything is missing.
