06. Step-by-Step Prompts

Copy and paste these into any free AI chat. Replace the bracketed parts with your own details.

Prompt 1: My first meal plan (beginner)

I'm new to using AI for meal planning. I want to plan [NUMBER] dinners for [PEOPLE] people.

Here's what I have in my pantry and fridge:
[PASTE YOUR LIST]

Please suggest a weekly menu. For each meal tell me:
- The dish name
- Which ingredients from my list it uses
- Approximate cook time
- 3-5 simple steps

Keep recipes simple (under 45 minutes, minimal cleanup). My budget for extra groceries is about $[AMOUNT].

Prompt 2: Pantry-first (use what you have)

I want to avoid food waste. Using only this ingredient list as much as possible, plan [NUMBER] dinners:
[PASTE YOUR LIST]

Tell me which ingredients will be left over after the week, and suggest one way to use each leftover.

Prompt 3: Grocery list from a menu

Based on this menu, make a grocery list of only what I don't already have.

I already have: [LIST]

Menu:
[PASTE MENU]

Organize by store section (produce, meat, dairy, canned, dry goods, frozen, bakery) with quantities for [PEOPLE] people.

Prompt 4: Dietary restrictions

Plan [NUMBER] dinners for [PEOPLE] people. I need [RESTRICTIONS, e.g. gluten-free and dairy-free]. I have: [LIST]. Flag any ingredient that might contain [RESTRICTION], and suggest safe swaps. Remind me to check labels myself.

Prompt 5: Budget focus

Plan [NUMBER] dinners for [PEOPLE] people with a total grocery budget of $[AMOUNT]. I already have: [LIST]. Use cheap staples (rice, beans, eggs, pasta, frozen vegetables). Include rough cost per meal and a grocery list. Prioritize meals with leftovers.

Prompt 6: Low-energy cooking

I'm low on energy today. Plan [NUMBER] dinners that use one pan or one sheet tray, minimal chopping, and produce leftovers for tomorrow's lunch. I have: [LIST].

Prompt 7: Leftover remix

I have leftover [FOOD]. Give me 3 completely different meals I can turn it into, each taking under 20 minutes.

Prompt 8: Snacks and breakfasts

Give me [NUMBER] breakfasts and [NUMBER] snacks under $[AMOUNT] each, using: [LIST]. No cooking required or minimal cooking.

How to revise (talk back to the AI)

The AI remembers your conversation. You can say:

  • "That's too complicated. Make it simpler."
  • "I don't like [ingredient]. Swap it for something else."
  • "Can you make this vegetarian?"
  • "Give me a version that takes 15 minutes instead of 45."
  • "Write this as a grocery list I can print."

Safety reminders

  • Double-check cooking times, especially for chicken, pork, and fish (use a meat thermometer: 165°F / 74°C for poultry).
  • AI can invent recipes that don't work. If something sounds strange, ask for a different idea.
  • AI is not a dietitian or doctor. For medical diets, confirm with your healthcare team.

Try this right now

Open a free AI chat and paste Prompt 1 with your real pantry list. See what comes back, then revise with one of the "talk back" lines above.
