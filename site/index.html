AI Meal Planning Guide

Plan a week of meals from what you already have, build grocery lists, honor dietary restrictions, and stick to a budget — using free AI tools. No purchase required, nothing to install.

Start here

Open a free AI chat (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Microsoft Copilot, or Meta AI), then copy and paste this:

I'm new to using AI for meal planning. I want to plan 5 dinners for 2 people.

Here's what I have in my pantry and fridge:
[PASTE YOUR LIST]

Please suggest a weekly menu. For each meal tell me:
- The dish name
- Which ingredients from my list it uses
- Approximate cook time
- 3-5 simple steps

Keep recipes simple (under 45 minutes, minimal cleanup). My budget for extra groceries is about $40.

Then tell it what you actually have and see what comes back.
